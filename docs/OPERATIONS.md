# Governed pricing optimizer

The supported surface is `/api/governed-pricing`. It accepts a tenant/idempotency-scoped, source-digested snapshot covering transactions, inventory, promotions, cost, competitor observations, demand drivers, and customer constraints. The deterministic engine estimates elasticity from observed price/quantity pairs, uses integer minor-unit prices, intersects margin, change, inventory, competitor, promotion, and segment constraints, applies one price across segments, and returns an explanation. New/sparse products are held; contradictory constraints return an explicit conflict. No language-model output can set a price.

Only pricing analysts create recommendations. A distinct pricing approver must attest before a future effective date is scheduled. A channel operator creates idempotent commerce/POS/ERP/inventory/CRM/market-data outbox work; reconciled provider versions and evidence advance state. Failures retry and dead-letter. Rollback is a separately evidenced, versioned channel operation restoring the recorded prior price. Snapshots, source evidence, approvals, evaluations, and events are append-only, and recommendation evidence cannot be rewritten.

Experiments require a nonzero holdout and treatment allocation, minimum samples, a recommendation digest, and metrics for incremental margin, conversion, churn, and cannibalization. Units are deterministically assigned from an experiment-scoped unit digest; raw customer identifiers are not persisted in the assignment table. Evaluation consumes authoritative counters and waits for delayed outcomes. Even a favorable result is only eligible for human approval; it never executes automatically.

Run bootstrap, migration, and the destructive legacy development seed only through their explicit scripts. The seed requires `SEED_ACK=reset-local-pricing-database` plus a runtime-only `DEMO_SEED_PASSWORD` of at least 12 characters and is always refused in production. Startup never installs dependencies, kills ports, creates/starts PostgreSQL, creates/migrates/seeds a database, or invents secrets. Review and back up before migration. Financial/pricing/legal review, fairness analysis, provider contracts/webhook authentication, real experiments, delayed-outcome windows, shock/promotion fixtures, security testing, channel reconciliation, restore rehearsal, and production monitoring remain launch gates.
